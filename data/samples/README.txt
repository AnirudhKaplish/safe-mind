Sample data for the Person report (Meridian dashboard -> "Person report (all posts)").

These are FICTIONAL people. The posts were written for demonstration only.
The files use the same columns as Reddit's own data export
(reddit.com/settings/data-request -> posts.csv / comments.csv).

  demo_person_struggling_posts.csv     + demo_person_struggling_comments.csv
      upload BOTH together: a student who starts well and gradually shows
      more signs of depression from March onwards (one post mentions wanting
      to disappear, which shows the urgent safety note).
  demo_person_doing_well_posts.csv
      a person with no signs of depression (green result).
